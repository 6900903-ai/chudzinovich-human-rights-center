# Wave 35 — Search authority and index quality

This wave strengthens the indexable CHUDO-owned layer without reopening scaled third-party source mirrors to indexing.

## Implemented

- multilingual editorial policy with verification, correction, privacy and automation boundaries;
- query-aligned titles and descriptions for high-value RU/BE/EN/PL hubs;
- Organization and WebSite structured identity on every localized homepage;
- stable publisher identity and author metadata;
- 1200×630 PNG social preview and 512×512 brand icon generated without third-party runtime dependencies;
- favicon, web app manifest, RSS discovery and localized Open Graph metadata;
- sitemap index covering the canonical page sitemap and fresh-news sitemap;
- `humans.txt` and RFC-style `/.well-known/security.txt` using public policy/contact URLs;
- final Pages-artifact enforcement for all authority assets and signals;
- daily read-only live SEO monitor for DNS, HTTPS, indexability, structured identity, sitemaps and preview assets.

## Preserved boundaries

- source-only Telegram and media detail pages remain `noindex,follow`;
- source archive and derivative pagination pages remain outside the canonical sitemap;
- empty people/prison catalogs remain `noindex,follow` until a real immutable snapshot is published;
- no analytics, ad technology, third-party font, remote script or visitor-runtime media request is introduced;
- structured data does not claim that source reports are independently verified CHUDO facts;
- no Search Console verification token is invented or committed.

## Ranking statement

This wave improves crawlability, entity consistency, snippet quality and public accountability. It cannot guarantee a particular Google position. Competitive rankings additionally require a non-empty verified human-rights database, original reports, durable citations and external references from reputable organizations and media.
